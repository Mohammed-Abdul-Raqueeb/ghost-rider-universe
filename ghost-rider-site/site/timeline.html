    1967 → 2028

    TIMELINE

    The road in order — publication milestones and the in-universe events they introduced. Larger marks are turning points.

    Scroll
    1967

    The Western Ghost Rider

    Ghost Rider #1 introduces Carter Slade, the glowing horseman of the Texas frontier. Seven issues.

    1972

    Johnny Blaze

    Marvel Spotlight #5 (August): stunt rider Johnny Blaze sells his soul to save Crash Simpson and becomes the flaming-skulled Ghost Rider.

    1973

    The first ongoing

    Ghost Rider vol. 2 #1 (September). The Orb debuts in Marvel Team-Up #15 the same year.

    1975

    The Champions

    Johnny joins Hercules, Black Widow, Angel and Iceman in Los Angeles.

    1982

    Centurious

    Ghost Rider #74 introduces the man without a soul; Zarathos is named soon after.

    1983

    Freedom

    Ghost Rider #81 ends the series with Zarathos trapped in the Crystal of Souls and Johnny Blaze human again.

    1989

    Blackheart

    Mephisto's son debuts in Daredevil #270.

    1990

    Danny Ketch

    Ghost Rider vol. 3 #1 (May): a new Rider, the Penance Stare, the Hell Cycle, Deathwatch and Blackout.

    1991

    Hearts of Darkness

    Ghost Rider, Wolverine and the Punisher versus Blackheart in Christ's Crown.

    1992

    Midnight Sons

    Lilith debuts (#28); Rise of the Midnight Sons; Vengeance (Badilino) debuts (#21); Spirits of Vengeance launches.

    1993

    Siege of Darkness

    Lilith and Zarathos assault the Midnight Sons across seventeen chapters.

    1994

    Ghost Rider 2099 · Blaze

    Zero Cochrane's cyberpunk Rider and Johnny's solo Blaze series both launch.

    1996

    Noble Kale

    Danny's Spirit is revealed as his ancestor; Blaze and Ketch are revealed as brothers.

    1998

    The Ketch series ends

    Ghost Rider vol. 3 #93 is the last published issue; #94 waits until 2007.

    2001

    The Hammer Lane

    Marvel Knights miniseries by Grayson and Kaniuga.

    2005

    Road to Damnation

    Ennis and Crain put Johnny on the endless highway of Hell.

    2006

    Lucifer

    Daniel Way's ongoing: Johnny escapes Hell with Lucifer's soul in 666 pieces.

    2007

    The film · Trail of Tears

    Ghost Rider (Mark Steven Johnson) opens in February with Nicolas Cage; Ennis's Civil War Rider debuts; Ghost Rider Finale publishes the lost #94.

    2008

    Zadkiel

    Jason Aaron's run begins at #20: the Spirits are Heaven's creations and the archangel is a traitor.

    2009

    Heaven's on Fire

    Blaze and Ketch lead the Riders against Zadkiel. The 2006 series ends at #35.

    2011

    Alejandra Jones

    The angel Adam gives Johnny's Spirit to a new host (Ghost Rider vol. 7).

    2012

    Spirit of Vengeance

    The second film (Neveldine/Taylor) opens in February; Cage's last ride.

    2014

    Robbie Reyes

    All-New Ghost Rider #1 (March): the Hell Charger arrives.

    2015

    Ghost Racers

    Secret Wars puts every Rider in Arcade's arena.

    2016

    Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. · Kushala

    Gabriel Luna's Robbie Reyes joins the MCU on television; Kushala debuts in Doctor Strange and the Sorcerers Supreme.

    2017

    Cosmic Ghost Rider · the First Rider

    Thanos #13 introduces Frank Castle's cosmic Rider; Marvel Legacy #1 introduces the mammoth-riding Ghost Rider of 1,000,000 BC.

    2018

    Damnation · Avengers

    Johnny Blaze takes the throne of Hell; Robbie Reyes joins Aaron's Avengers.

    2019

    King of Hell

    Ed Brisson's Ghost Rider: Danny versus King Johnny.

    2021

    Robbie in Hell

    Robbie briefly claims the throne to expel Eli; Ghost Rider: Kushala Infinite Comic.

    2022

    Percy · Midnight Suns

    Benjamin Percy's Ghost Rider (April); Marvel's Midnight Suns video game with Robbie as playable Rider (December).

    2023

    Weapons of Vengeance

    Ghost Rider/Wolverine crossover introduces Bagra-ghul; Danny Ketch: Ghost Rider miniseries.

    2024

    Final Vengeance

    Frank Castle takes the Spirit; Spirits of Vengeance begins; Robbie Reyes Special.

    2025

    Spirits of Violence

    Ghost Rider vs. Galactus one-shot (June); Spirits of Violence #1 (October) opens the Violent Era.

    2026

    Ryan Gosling

    San Diego Comic-Con, July: Marvel Studios announces Ghost Rider with Ryan Gosling, directed by Shawn Levy; dated July 28, 2028 in August.

    2028

    The Marvel Studios film

    Scheduled for July 28, 2028.
