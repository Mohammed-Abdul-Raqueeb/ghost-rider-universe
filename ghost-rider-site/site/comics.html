    The second wave

    Road to Damnation and Trail of Tears (Ennis/Crain) — two self-contained painted miniseries, both collected together in the Ghost Rider by Garth Ennis Omnibus.

    Hearts of Darkness and Rise of the Midnight Sons — the 1990s at full volume; the Danny Ketch Epic Collections vols. 2–6 (2025–26) cover the whole Midnight Sons era, with a Danny Ketch Omnibus vol. 3 scheduled for January 2027.

    Cosmic Ghost Rider (Cates/Shaw) — Thanos #13–18 and the 2018 miniseries, collected as Cosmic Ghost Rider: Baby Thanos Must Die.

    Ghost Rider by Benjamin Percy — the 2022–24 run plus Weapons of Vengeance, Final Vengeance and Vengeance Forever, with an Omnibus solicited for October 2026.

    Fifty years, ten volumes, one road. Start anywhere the fire catches.

    DIGITAL

    Marvel Unlimited

    Almost every issue mentioned on this site is on Marvel Unlimited, including the Infinite Comics (Ghost Rider: Kushala, 2021) that never saw print. Reading-order guides such as Crushing Krisis maintain issue-by-issue listings that are updated as new collections are solicited.
